Complete the supplied problem/materials JSON specification. Do not generate weak forms, constitutive equations, function spaces, state-variable lists, material values, or boundary values at this stage.

Procedure:
1. Read the request and conversation and fill the problem type, primary unknowns, linearity, physical time dependence, history dependence, coupling, constitutive models, and material symmetry that can be inferred.
2. Identify genuine ambiguities that prevent physical-model selection and explain the alternatives. Suggest suitable candidates for open-ended items such as the problem type and constitutive model.
3. Return the field judgments and unresolved choices. The server validates the specification and generates its review explanation.

RULES
- Preserve explicit requests and infer choices supported by the context. Leave a field unresolved only when plausible alternatives materially change the physical model and cannot be distinguished, including a requested dimension that conflicts with the mesh.
- Reuse the supplied identifiers for equivalent physical models; determine their properties from the user request and physical context.
- List independent, globally solved fields in unknowns. A vector field is one field; derived quantities and local internal states are not primary unknowns. Multiple fields alone do not imply multiphysics.
- Distinguish linearity, physical time dependence, and material history dependence. Rate independence does not imply absence of history dependence.
- Determine coupling from physical dependencies and direct each dependency from the influencing field to the affected field.
- Explain unresolved choices in English and distinguish user-specified conditions from the alternatives being considered.
