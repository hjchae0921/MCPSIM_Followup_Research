1. Steady State Heat Transfer
A total heat load of 48 W is uniformly applied to the entire bottom surface of the attached heat sink, while the remaining exposed surfaces are in contact with air at 20°C. 
I would like to see the temperature distribution. 
The heat sink is made of aluminum.

2. Linear Elastic
I would like to fix the left end face of the attached metal tensile specimen, and slowly pull the right end face by 0.1 mm in the longitudinal direction. 
I would like to observe the resulting deformation.

3. Thermoelastic
I would like to heat the right end of the attached metal I-beam while keeping both end faces fixed in place. The left end is attached to a support maintained at 20°C, a total heat load is uniformly applied across the entire right end face, and the side surfaces are insulated to prevent heat loss. 
I would like to observe the temperature distribution and the resulting thermal deformation.

4. Piezoelectric
I would like to investigate how the attached cantilever beam, made of two layers of piezoelectric material, bends when a voltage is applied. 
The left end face is fixed to prevent any displacement, and a voltage of 0 V is applied to the bottom electrode, while 10 V is applied to the top electrode. 
No mechanical forces are applied to the remaining surfaces. 
I would like to determine the tip displacement, overall deformation, and internal electric potential distribution under a constant applied voltage.

5. Thermoelectric
I would like to investigate the electric current generated by a temperature difference in the attached thermoelectric device consisting of p-type and n-type semiconductor legs and their connecting components. 
Please maintain the upper heating surface at 80°C and the lower ends at 20°C, while ensuring that no heat escapes through the side surfaces.
Electrically connect the two lower electrodes using a resistive wire. 
I would like to observe the temperature and electric potential distributions after sufficient time has passed to reach steady state. 
Please also account for the heat generated by the electric current.

6. Hyperelastic
I would like to fix the bottom surface of the attached rubber bellows and slowly compress it by pushing the top surface downward by 8 mm. 
Please neglect contact between adjacent folds and any time-dependent material behavior. 
I would like to observe the deformation resulting from the compression.
